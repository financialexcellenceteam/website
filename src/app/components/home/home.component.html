What You'll Learn

Banking

An overview of how banks work, including account types, safety protections, digital services, and practical skills for choosing and using banking tools responsibly to build financial confidence

Credit cards

This topic covers how credit cards work, including interest, fees, limits, and responsible use. Students learn to avoid debt, compare cards, build credit, and use cards wisely

Budget and money management

Learn to budget, save, invest, and spend wisely through practical strategies that build financial confidence, reduce stress, and support long-term stability and responsible decision-making

Inflation and compound interest

“Explore how inflation reduces purchasing power while compound interest grows money over time, helping students understand long-term financial impacts, real value, and smarter saving and investing decisions

Investment

Learn how to build wealth through saving and investing, understanding risk, returns, diversification, and strategies that support long-term goals, financial security, and smarter money decisions

Loans

Understand how loans work, including interest, terms, and repayment strategies, helping students borrow responsibly, avoid harmful debt, compare options, and make informed decisions about major financial commitments

Taxes

Learn how taxes work, including income tax, withholding, forms, and basic deductions, helping students understand paychecks, obligations, and how taxes impact overall financial planning and decisions

Insurance

Learn how insurance protects finances from unexpected events by understanding premiums, deductibles, coverage types, and how managing risk supports long-term stability and financial security

Fraud, Scam and Financial security

Learn how to recognize scams, protect personal information, use secure banking practices, and respond to fraud to keep accounts, identity, and financial data safe in everyday life
